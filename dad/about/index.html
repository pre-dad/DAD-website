About DAD

Built for people who stopped believing.

DAD stands for Dynamic Aid Delivery. We're a non-profit that funds, runs and proves its own programs, in public, as it happens. The most efficient charity to ever exist.

A letter from our founder

Growing up poor, I watched my grandmother and our neighbors struggle while resources and help were hard to come by, non-existent, or barely enough. As a single father since the age of 16, I have seen firsthand what accessing resources is like. There are good charities and good people out there; however, people are still in need. I've lost everything before and been homeless with a child. I've been to food banks and seen the low-quality food provided, and I've spoken to others about how slow or broken these systems are. I know firsthand that we can do better!

I've also spent the last decade building companies. Closing contracts, opening offices, hiring teams. In business, you always know where the money went. It's on a screen. Somebody's accountable for every line.

Then I looked at giving. Americans handed over $617 billion last year, more than ever. And year after year, fewer people are giving. Not because they stopped caring. Because they stopped believing it worked. I was one of them. I know what efficiency looks like, and just how much work can be done in a week. I know what can be accomplished, and now I know that the system is broken and the need for DAD is bigger than ever.

Every area of human suffering increases year over year. The problem isn't generosity. It's delivery. The machinery between your dollar and the work was built before the internet, and the machinery is where the money goes.

So we're building the version that should have existed all along. A non-profit that runs its own programs, tracks every dollar to what it did, and puts it in public where anyone can check. No glossy annual report. No "trust us." Just the record, and massive execution.

You don't have to take our word for it. Watch us. Join us.

Chris Watley, Founder & CEO

What we are

  • A better operating model for how generosity becomes results
  • The owner and operator of the programs your money funds
  • An organization that funds, runs, verifies and publishes in one motion
  • A model we intend to hand to the whole sector once it's proven

What we're not

  • Another charity fighting over the same donations
  • A middleman that passes your money on and disappears
  • An app that sends cash to individuals
  • A replacement for organizations with decades of field expertise. We bring them in.
  • Pretending technology fixes everything

The plan

  1. 1 · Build the system.
  2. 2 · Prove it through focused initiatives.
  3. 3 · Scale what works.

The vision can be enormous. The first steps have to be achievable.

Once it works, we hand it over. Open source. An operating system for modern giving, open to every organization that wants it.

Our measure of success isn't how big DAD gets. It's how much less the world needs it.
