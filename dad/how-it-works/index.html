How it works

We collect resources at scale. Then strategically deploy.

And you watch it the whole way.

Fund Operate Prove Grow

  1. 01 · Fund.

    Donors give about a dollar a day. Partners bring capital. It all lands in program budgets.

  2. 02 · Operate.

    We design and run the programs ourselves. If someone's already cracked the problem, we bring them in and build it together.

  3. 03 · Prove.

    What it funded, what it delivered and what it cost. Recorded as it happens. Published as it happens. Like a living documentary.

  4. 04 · Grow.

    Proof does what promises can't. People see it work, bring their people, and fund more programs.

HOME Initiatives.

Funded by donors. Programs DAD designs and owns, across food, health, opportunities and infrastructure.

Corporate Initiatives.

Funded by partners. Built with a brand from the ground up, or sponsored after the community votes for it. Either way, DAD runs it and the numbers are public.

Community Programs.

Funded by both. Run in cities by DAD Impact Teams and volunteers.

  3. Convene.

    We bring in the professors, practitioners and field experts who've solved it before or know how.

  4. Run.

    We operate it ourselves, and the community gets a vote on what comes next.

Nobody gives to a charity because it uses AI. They give because they believe it'll help more people. AI is how we get there. It's never the reason to care.

What AI does here

  • Makes checking cheap enough to run on every outcome, not a sample.
  • Matches need to resources without a committee or a quarter's delay.
  • Removes the paperwork layer that normally eats the donation.
  • Turns reporting into a byproduct of the work, instead of a document someone writes.

What stays human

  • Strategy, ethics, safeguarding and every decision that matters.
  • Relationships with members, partners, creators and volunteers.
  • Judgment in the field, where context beats data.
  • Accountability. A model can't be accountable. A person can.

By design: no AI agent goes live without a named human owner, a written scope, an escalation path and a kill switch.
