01 · The problem

$617,200,000,000

Given by Americans last year. Up 5%.

And millions of people stopped giving.

3.6% fewer donors. Fourth year running.

They didn't want to stop helping. They stopped trusting the institutions.

Because they found out things like..

In New York alone:

$187 million.

Four cancer charities. Sued by the FTC and all fifty states.

86 cents of every dollar.

02 · How it works

We create a movement of the people.

Millions of people pool together a dollar a day. It goes into a program created by experts backed by real data. And you watch it the whole way.

  1. 01 · Fund.

    Your dollar lands in a fund, alongside millions of others, with our partners' resources and capital too.

  2. 02 · Operate.

    We run it ourselves. If someone's already cracked the problem, we bring them in. We never hand off the money and walk away. We are a bunch of nerds dedicated to solving the problem with data, and love.

  3. 03 · Prove.

    Every dollar on the record. What it funded. What it delivered. What it cost. Published as it happens on our public ledger, not a year later. We also video the progress, stream, and share weekly.

  4. 04 · Grow.

    Proof spreads faster than promises. People see it work. They bring their people. More programs get funded.

Programs create proof. Proof builds trust. Trust brings people. People fund programs.
